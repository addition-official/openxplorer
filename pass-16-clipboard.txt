QUESTION: (1) Closing the last window now leaves the process running, without a window, while it owns the clipboard (CLIP-010). It ends once another app copies something, or a paste empties a cut, and Quit/--quit end it at once. Windows behaves this way (Explorer's process outlives its windows), and without it copied files are lost on GNOME, Weston and plain X11 (KDE's Klipper already keeps them).
