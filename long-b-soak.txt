LEAKS AND DEFECTS FOUND AND FIXED
1. Zombie picture decoders (aaefe16d). At every idle stretch the upstream
   build kept 3-4 dead bwrap children. Cause, confirmed with strace in a
   separate instance: bubblewrap's --die-with-parent (PR_SET_PDEATHSIG) kills
   the sandbox when the *thread* that started it ends; the decoder pool was
   filled from GIO worker threads that retire after an idle spell, so idle
   decoders were SIGKILLed at once and stayed unreaped in the pool until the
   next picture failed on them and started a new sandbox. Fix: decoders are
   started from one long-lived thread; a dead idle decoder is detected with
   waitid(WNOWAIT) and dropped (reaped) instead of being handed a picture.
   Guard tests: a_pooled_decoder_outlives_the_thread_that_started_it,
   a_decoder_that_died_while_idle_is_replaced_not_refused (the test decoder
   now dies with its starting thread, as bubblewrap's does). After the fix the
   soak shows no zombies; the idle pool keeps four live decoders (~46 MiB PSS
   in all), which is what the pool was meant to do.
2. Stale "Today at" dates after midnight (4d9379d5, test fix 5178f87b).
   Cause: follow_the_day regrouped grouped views but never redrew the date
   cells, and an ungrouped view was not touched at all. Fix: the details view
   remembers the day its relative dates were written from and redraws the
   date columns when the window follows the day; the cells use the same clock
   as the groups (ox-core column_date_text_at). Guard tests:
   relative_dates_move_on_when_the_day_changes,
   relative_dates_under_the_groups_move_on_when_the_day_changes.
