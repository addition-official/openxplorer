ROOT CAUSE, so far
- Not the codepoints: the command bar asks for exactly Windows' list (Cut E8C6, Copy E8C8, Paste E77F, Rename E8AC, Share E72D, Delete E74D, Sort E8CB, More E712, Back E72B, ...); New asks for AddTo ECC8, the circled plus, because Windows' New inks 15x15, which ECC8 gives at a 16-pixel em (you measured) and E710 does not (13x13).
- Not the setting or the timing: preferences.symbolFont and useIconFiles are read at startup and on every change (app_context follows the settings), menus are built when they open, after the font was read, and every image already shown is drawn again when the font is read.
- Not the path: a font in ~/.local/share/fonts is read and drawn (the window test now chooses it there). The app reads the file itself; it never registers the font with fontconfig or Pango, so the family name ("Segoe Fluent Icons") does not matter and no sandbox or portal is involved (the deb is not sandboxed).
- Not the icon theme here: the glyphs are written as symbolic SVGs into ~/.cache/winspace/windows-glyphs/<key>/hicolor/scalable/actions and that folder is added to GTK's icon theme search path; the system hicolor index.theme here lists scalable/actions, as Debian's and Ubuntu's do.
- Most likely: the reader refuses the user's SegoeIcons.ttf as a whole (if its outlines are CFF, "OTTO", or a table the reader requires is missing), and until now it said nothing: every glyph fell back to the app's own and the row said the font had none of the glyphs.
