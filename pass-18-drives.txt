Still not confirmed from a Microsoft source: 4 to 9, 11 and 12. Use a USB stick you can erase (exFAT or NTFS) in the Windows 11 VM's File Explorer.

1. (DONE, matched) Pulling a stick out during a copy onto it
   Steps: Copy a folder of about 2,000 small files from Documents to the stick; while the progress window runs, pull the stick out (or, in the VM, detach the USB device).
   Look for: the exact title and text of the question (OpenXplorer: "Interrupted Action", "An unexpected error is keeping you from copying the folder. ...", then "The device is not ready."), the error code shown (for example 0x80070015 or 0x800701B1), and whether Skip asks again for every later file.

2. (DONE, matched) The window showing the stick when it is pulled out
   Steps: Open the stick's root in Explorer, then pull it out.
   Look for: whether the window closes, goes to This PC, or stays with a message. OpenXplorer shows "This location is unavailable / The drive or network share that holds this folder was disconnected." with Try again.

3. (DONE, matched) Opening a drive that is not ready
   Steps: Type the letter of a removed stick, for example E:\, into the address bar.
   Look for: the exact dialog title and text (OpenXplorer's page: "E:\ is not accessible. The device is not ready." style, with Details).

4. Address bar and Up inside a stick
   Steps: Open a folder two levels deep on the stick; look at the address bar; press Alt+Up until you leave the stick.
   Look for: the crumbs (OpenXplorer: This PC > <stick name> > Folder > Folder) and where Up goes from the stick's root (OpenXplorer: This PC).

5. Renaming a stick
   Steps: In This PC, right-click the stick > Rename (and try F2 on its tile), type "Trip: 2026 photos", press Enter; then repeat while a file on the stick is open in Notepad.
   Look for: whether it renames in place, what it does with ":" and a long name on exFAT/NTFS, whether it asks for administrator permission, and the message while a file is open. OpenXplorer opens a "Rename <drive>" dialog and refuses a drive in use with "The drive is in use. Close the files and programs using it, then try again."

6. Deleting on a stick
   Steps: Select a file on the stick and press Delete.
   Look for: the exact question (expected "Are you sure you want to permanently delete this file?") and whether it goes to the Recycle Bin. OpenXplorer moves it to the stick's own trash without asking (see QUESTION).

7. Format
   Steps: Right-click the stick > Format..., pick exFAT, label "Photos", Quick Format, Start.
   Look for: the dialog's fields (Capacity, File system, Allocation unit size, Restore device defaults, Volume label, Quick Format), the warning's exact text, and the message when it ends ("Format Complete."?). Also right-click C: and look for whether Format is offered and what it says.

8. Drive Properties
   Steps: Right-click the stick > Properties.
   Look for: the tabs (General, Tools, Hardware, Sharing, ReadyBoost, Customize?), the Type text ("USB Drive" or "Removable Disk"), whether the label box on General can be edited and Apply renames, and Tools > Error checking.

9. Plugging a stick in
   Steps: Plug the stick in with Explorer open on This PC.
   Look for: the AutoPlay notification's exact text and choices, whether This PC and the navigation pane update at once, and the stick's icon and name ("USB Drive (E:)" for one with no label).

10. (DONE, matched) Eject while a program uses the stick
   Steps: Open a file on the stick in Notepad, then right-click the stick in the navigation pane > Eject.
   Look for: the exact title and text, and whether it names the program. OpenXplorer's Eject shows GTK's "Volume is busy" with the program's name (sleep, in the test), Cancel, Unmount Anyway and End Process.

11. A write-protected stick or SD card
   Steps: Set the card's lock switch (or a USB stick with a write-protect switch); try New > Folder and Delete on it.
   Look for: the exact messages. OpenXplorer keeps the commands on and shows the system's error, as DEV-015 decided.

12. A stick with several partitions
   Steps: Plug in a stick with two or three partitions.
   Look for: whether each gets its own letter in This PC and the pane, and whether Eject on one removes them all.
