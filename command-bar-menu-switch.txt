Earlier report for the feature (at 57d41dbc) follows unchanged.
Design: the command bar's menus no longer hold a pointer grab (autohide off), so every click reaches what is under it, as in Windows 11 (switch on one click, click-through on files); the window's existing press handler closes the open menu and leaves a press on a menu's own button to that button, and one module (command_bar/menu_group.rs) owns which bar menu is open. No timers, idle callbacks or global state; on X11 the module takes a press on the open menu that GTK would misroute after a relayout (GTK 4.14 gdk_surface_ensure_motion).
