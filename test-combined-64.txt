CHANGES (everything since deb 63, in plain words):
- Google Drive can be changed, as an opt-in (gdrive-write): Settings > Files & folders > Google Drive has a new switch, "Allow changes to Google Drive", off by default. On, copies, moves, renames, deletes, New, Paste and drops reach Google Drive through GVfs; deleting there removes items for good (they do not go to Drive's trash). Off, Google Drive stays read-only, now also enforced by the file layer itself; OneDrive stays read-only either way. Behind it: uploads are written beside their final name and only published by a rename once their size checks out, so an interrupted or incomplete copy never appears; Replace goes through reversible renames and is refused when two items share the title; Google Docs, Sheets and Slides are refused with a clear message, as they exist only online; a full drive or a lost connection is reported in Windows' words.
