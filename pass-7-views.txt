5. Tiles details by file type. In Tiles, look at the lines under the name of an mp3, a jpg, a pdf, an mp4 and a folder. Note which details show (type and size, or artist and album, or dimensions). Ours shows type and size for every file, and the name alone for a folder or zip file (as ref-16 shows).
6. Content details by file type. In Content, look at the same five items. Note the second line and the details at the right (for example Length for media, Date taken and Dimensions for pictures). Ours shows nothing under the name (as ref-17 shows for documents), then Date modified and Size at the right.
7. Empty folder focus. Open an empty folder and press Tab until the file area has the keyboard. Look at whether "This folder is empty." gets a focus outline. Ours draws one (ACC-016).
8. Compact view in the View menu. Open View. Look at whether Compact view is a top-level row (between Content and Show) or inside Show. Ours puts it inside Show.
9. Header menu wording. Right-click a column title in Details. Note the exact wording and capitals of the first two rows ("Size Column to Fit" or "Size column to fit") and which columns are listed before "More...".
10. List scrolling. In List with 10,000 files, scroll sideways with the scrollbar arrows and with Page Down. Look at whether the leftmost column is ever cut in half or always starts whole. Ours scrolls by pixels, so a column can be cut at the left edge.
11. Group headings in icon views. Group by Type in Large icons. Compare the heading's size, chevron and gap above the first row with our capture after-large-grouped-light. Our headings follow the Details headings (15 px title, 8 px gap above).
12. List with groups. In grouped List (Group by Type, Ctrl+Shift+5), click a group title, then double-click it. Look at whether a click selects the group's items and whether a double-click does anything. Ours selects the items on a click and never collapses a List group.
13. Small icons heading line. In grouped Small icons, look at whether the line under a heading runs the full window width or only as wide as the columns. Ours runs as wide as the columns.
14. List heading alignment. In grouped List, look at whether each group's title starts level with its items' icons or further left. Ours starts about 24 px left of the icons.
15. View switch scroll. In a long folder in Large icons, select nothing, press Ctrl+Shift+4 and Ctrl+Shift+6 in turn, then select one item far down and switch again. Look at whether the view stays at the top with nothing selected and scrolls only enough to show the selected item, and which item gets the dotted focus outline. Ours starts at the top, scrolls only for a selected item, and puts the keyboard on the first selected item, else the first item.
16. Long names on Home. Open a file with a 120-character name, then look at Home's Recent list at full screen and in a narrow window. Look at where the name is cut, the gap before Date accessed, and whether hovering shows the whole name. Ours cuts it at the Name column (484 px at full width) with "..." and a 12 px gap, and shows the whole name on hover.
17. (done: answered with a 775 px row) Content at another width. In Content (with Compact view on), make the window about 1200 px wide and capture it like ref-17. Measure where "Date modified:" starts from the row's left edge. If it is still 674 px, the column is fixed; if it is near 38.6% of the row's width, it is proportional.
18. Small icons row height. In Small icons with Compact view on, measure the distance between rows. ref-14 shows 21 px.
