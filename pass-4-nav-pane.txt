VM CHECK
Windows 11 behaviors still not confirmed. Try each in the Windows 11 VM's File Explorer.

1. Pin from a menu goes last
   Steps: Right-click any folder in the file list > Pin to Quick access.
   Look for: it appears at the bottom of the pinned folders. OpenXplorer now does that.

2. A file dragged near a pin's edge
   Steps: Drag a .pdf from Documents onto the very top edge of the pinned "Downloads" row, where a folder would show the insertion line.
   Look for: an insertion line, or "Move to Downloads" with the whole row highlighted, and where the file ends up. OpenXplorer moves it into Downloads.

3. Dragging the pane's edge
   Steps: Drag the border between the navigation pane and the files wider, close Explorer and open it again; then drag the border fully left.
   Look for: the width is kept, and whether the pane collapses to nothing or stops at a minimum width (OpenXplorer stops at about 120 px).

4. Ejecting a drive that's in use
   Steps: Plug in a USB stick, open a file from it in Notepad and keep it open; show the stick's root in Explorer, then right-click the stick in the navigation pane > Eject.
   Look for: the exact title and text of the message (OpenXplorer: "This device is currently in use. Close any programs or windows that might be using the device, and then try again."), and whether the window stays on the stick.

5. Ejecting a drive that's free
   Steps: Close Notepad, show the stick's root, then Eject from the navigation pane.
   Look for: where the window goes (This PC, closes, or stays with an error). OpenXplorer goes to This PC.

6. A computer under Network
   Steps: Expand Network in the navigation pane, then expand a DESKTOP-... computer with its chevron, and press Right and Left on it.
   Look for: whether its shared folders appear under it, whether it asks for a password first for a computer that needs one, whether Network starts expanded or collapsed when Explorer opens, and whether expanding Network shows the computers at once or after a moment.

7. Keypad and main keyboard plus and minus
   Steps: Select "This PC" in the navigation pane; press the main keyboard's "-" and "+", then the keypad's "-", "+" and "*".
   Look for: which keys collapse and expand. OpenXplorer uses only the keypad keys.

8. Home and Gallery menus
   Steps: Right-click Home, then Gallery, in the navigation pane.
   Look for: the exact items and order. OpenXplorer now offers Open in new tab and Open in new window there.

9. Unpin message
   Steps: Right-click a pinned folder > Unpin from Quick access.
   Look for: whether any message appears. OpenXplorer shows "Unpinned. The folder was not deleted." (kept by the owner for now).

10. A pinned folder that was deleted
   Steps: Pin a new folder, delete it, then click its pin.
   Look for: whether Windows removes the pin, shows a dialog (and its wording), or shows an in-window message. OpenXplorer keeps the pin and shows "This location is unavailable" in the window.

11. Screen reader names
   Steps: Turn on Narrator (Ctrl+Win+Enter), Tab into the navigation pane and arrow over a pinned folder, This PC, a drive and a computer under Network.
   Look for: what Narrator says for each, for example "pinned", collapsed or expanded, and the tree level.
