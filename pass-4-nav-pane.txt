VISIBLE CHANGES (pass-4-tree-expand)
- Every mounted drive under This PC (Local Disk included) and every network share under Network (a saved location, or a share inside an expanded computer) has a chevron before its icon. Clicking it lists the drive's or share's folders under it; clicking again hides them. Right on the row lists them (the keyboard stays on the row), Right again moves to the first folder, Left moves back to the parent, Left on an expanded row collapses it. A double-click on the row opens it and lists its folders.
- Folders are listed by name (ignoring case), without files, and without hidden folders unless hidden items are shown. Each folder that holds folders has its own chevron. On a local disk, that is checked on a worker thread right after listing, stopping at the first folder found. On a network share the chevron shows until the folder is expanded, as in Windows. Each level is 16 px further in than its parent; a row with a chevron keeps its icon in its level's column.
- Listing is off the main loop (GIO's asynchronous listing). A share that needs mounting is mounted first, asking for a password only if it wants one. A folder that cannot be read collapses again. At most 1,000 folders are listed under one folder; then a row "More folders in {name}…" opens the folder.
- With "Expand to current folder" on (the pane's empty-space menu, off by default), the trees open down to the folder the tab shows, one level as each listing arrives, and highlight it. Expanding or collapsing anything by hand stops that until the next navigation.
- The row with the keyboard keeps it when the pane's rows are redrawn.
- Tooltips: see the table below. Navigation pane rows show a tooltip only while the name is cut with "…", and then the full name; the chevrons, the network glyph, the capacity bars and the resize handle have none; the Details button shows "Show or hide the details pane.".
