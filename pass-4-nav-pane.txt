TOOLTIP CRASH (pass-4-tooltip-crash)
Cause, read from GTK 4.14.5's gtktooltip.c, gtkwindow.c and gtkpopover.c and reproduced:
- GTK keeps one tooltip per display. Every pointer motion or enter points it at that event's surface, a popover's included, and restarts its 500 ms timer, whether or not the widget there has a tooltip.
- gtk_window_destroy lets go of the tooltip only if it points at that window (gtk_tooltip_unset_surface). Nothing lets go of it for a popover.
- If the popover is freed, a weak reference clears the tooltip, so that is safe. But GTK 4.14 keeps a popover alive after its window when the popover's button is hidden after something in the popover had the keyboard. That also happens with a plain GtkPopover holding a focused GtkButton, so it is GTK's own, not our MenuPopover's. The crashing test does exactly this: Places menu open, rows get the keyboard, popdown, then win.sidebar shows the pane again, which hides the Places button, then the window is destroyed.
- When the pointer was last over that menu and the window is destroyed before GTK hears of the pointer over the window again, the timer fires on the popover, which has no surface: gdk_surface_get_device_position(NULL), the critical, SIGTRAP in tests. Under the test harness the pointer is left wherever an earlier test put it, so the crash depends on the order and the window placement. It passes alone, and it did not show in my full local run (2903 tests): there the pointer rested at (720, 500), outside the Places menu (x 159-469, y 64-602).
Fix: new module tooltip_guard.rs. When a popover closes (GtkPopover::closed) in a window that is still shown, the app calls gtk_widget_trigger_tooltip_query on the window. GTK queries the pointer (on X11 a round trip, after the popover's unmap), finds the window under it, and points the tooltip there; the window's destruction then lets go of it. A popover closed because its window is being hidden or destroyed is left alone (asking then would point the tooltip back at a window GTK has just let go of). Attached to MenuPopover (every app menu), ChoiceList, the info bubbles, address bar suggestions, the protocol chooser, root places, the rename balloon and the archive dialog's info popover. Not to settings_page/file_icons.rs (the icon-files feature, left untouched as asked).
No visible change. No new texts.
Not covered: on Wayland GDK answers the pointer query from the last events it has, so the call may do nothing there if the window goes before the popover's leave event arrives. Popovers GTK builds itself (drop-down lists, entry completion, emoji chooser) are not covered; the app has no GtkDropDown or PopoverMenu. A popover still open when its window is destroyed is not covered either: GTK lets go of the window's tooltip before it closes the popover, and no public call can clear it then.

VISIBLE CHANGES (pass-4-tree-expand)
- Every mounted drive under This PC (Local Disk included) and every network share under Network (a saved location, or a share inside an expanded computer) has a chevron before its icon. Clicking it lists the drive's or share's folders under it; clicking again hides them. Right on the row lists them (the keyboard stays on the row), Right again moves to the first folder, Left moves back to the parent, Left on an expanded row collapses it. A double-click on the row opens it and lists its folders.
- Folders are listed by name (ignoring case), without files, and without hidden folders unless hidden items are shown. Each folder that holds folders has its own chevron. On a local disk, that is checked on a worker thread right after listing, stopping at the first folder found. On a network share the chevron shows until the folder is expanded, as in Windows. Each level is 16 px further in than its parent; a row with a chevron keeps its icon in its level's column.
- Listing is off the main loop (GIO's asynchronous listing). A share that needs mounting is mounted first, asking for a password only if it wants one. A folder that cannot be read collapses again. At most 1,000 folders are listed under one folder; then a row "More folders in {name}…" opens the folder.
- With "Expand to current folder" on (the pane's empty-space menu, off by default), the trees open down to the folder the tab shows, one level as each listing arrives, and highlight it. Expanding or collapsing anything by hand stops that until the next navigation.
- The row with the keyboard keeps it when the pane's rows are redrawn.
- Tooltips: see the table below. Navigation pane rows show a tooltip only while the name is cut with "…", and then the full name; the chevrons, the network glyph, the capacity bars and the resize handle have none; the Details button shows "Show or hide the details pane.".
