- System title bar, switched at once (pass-14-system-decorations, 5c9c0324): turning on "Use system window decorations" now changes windows that are already open, not only new ones, so on KDE Plasma (KWin) open windows get the desktop's own title bar right away. Checked by its author on KWin Wayland and Mutter Wayland.
- Window menu on the title bar (pass-14-system-decorations, 5c9c0324): right-clicking the empty part of the title bar, or of the tab row under the desktop's frame, opens Windows 11's window menu: Restore, Move, Size, Minimize, Maximize, Close (Alt+F4), then a check item "Use system title bar and borders" that turns the same setting on or off. - Submenus close when the pointer leaves (classic-menu-windows, 415f2801): with a submenu open (such as Send to), moving the pointer off every menu closes the submenu after the 400 ms menu delay, as in Windows 11, while the menu it came from stays open until a click outside or Escape.   - Send to, Share with, Create shortcut and Compressed (zipped) folder now work on a selected Recent file; Share with used to be grayed.
